# Commercial licensing strategy

BlueberryMe v0.1.0 is distributed under **GPL-3.0-only**.

GPLv3 does not prohibit commercial use. Anyone may charge money for GPL-covered software or services while complying with the GPL.

The project also intentionally preserves the option for the copyright holder to offer future releases under a separate proprietary/commercial licence (dual licensing). To keep that option legally viable, copyright ownership and contribution rights must be controlled. External source contributions should therefore not be merged until a suitable contributor licence agreement (CLA) has been adopted and signed.

A future commercial licence could, for example, permit proprietary embedding, redistribution without GPL source obligations, enterprise support, certified builds, or additional enterprise modules. This document itself does not grant such a proprietary licence.

If hosted/SaaS copyleft becomes strategically important, AGPL-3.0 can be evaluated before accepting outside contributions. Changing licence strategy later is straightforward only while the required copyright rights remain under common control.

This is project licensing guidance, not legal advice.
